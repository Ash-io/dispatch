Guides

Every Dispatch feature, step by step. Each guide follows the same steps as its how-to video.

Getting started

  • Install Dispatch and send your first requestDownload the app, skip the sign-up, and get a real response back in under a minute.
  • Organise requests with collections and foldersGroup requests into collections and folders, share settings across them, and document them.
  • Environments and variablesWrite a request once, then point it at sandbox or production with one switch.
  • Keep secrets in the VaultAPI keys and passwords that never sync, never export, and never leak into scripts.
  • Authorization: tokens, keys and OAuth 2.0Set auth once for a whole collection, or override it on a single request.

Testing and automation

  • Scripts and testsChain tokens between requests and check every response, with Postman's pm API.
  • Run a whole collectionRun every request in order, repeat it with data files, and see a pass or fail matrix.
  • Load test an endpointThrow up to 500 virtual users at a request and watch latency, throughput and errors live.
  • Examples and mock serversSave responses as examples, then serve them from a local mock server.
  • Compare a request across environmentsSend one request to staging and production at once, and see exactly what differs.
  • Find, filter and diff responsesSearch a body, filter JSON with JSONPath, save examples, and diff against earlier responses.

Protocols

  • Live WebSocket connectionsOpen a socket, watch frames stream in, and send messages back.
  • Call gRPC servicesLoad services by reflection or .proto files, write the message as JSON, and invoke.
  • GraphQL with autocompleteLoad the schema, get field suggestions and checks as you type, and explore the API.

Productivity

  • The AI assistantLet Claude write tests from a real response, explain results, and draft requests from plain English.
  • Import, export, cURL and codeBring in Postman, OpenAPI, Insomnia or HAR files, paste cURL, and turn requests into code.
  • Keep collections in GitSave a collection as readable files, commit it with your code, and review changes in pull requests.
  • Run collections from the command lineRun the same collections in a terminal or in CI, with reports and a proper exit code.
  • Work faster: palette, history and cookiesJump anywhere with the command palette, reopen past sends, and manage cookies.

Postman and teams

  • Two-way sync with PostmanWork in the same workspaces as teammates who use Postman, in both directions.
  • Set up a Dispatch server for your teamRun dispatch-server on your own machine, Docker, or Cloudflare, and claim it as admin.
  • Connect Postman and give your team accessAdd Postman API keys, invite people with roles, and decide who reaches which workspace.
  • Leave Postman: workspaces on your serverCreate workspaces stored on the Dispatch server itself, or copy your Postman workspaces over.
  • Connect the desktop app to your serverSign in to your Dispatch server from the app, then follow every change in the audit log.
