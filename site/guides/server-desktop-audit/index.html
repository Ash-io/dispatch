Connect the desktop app to your server

Sign in to your Dispatch server from the app, then follow every change in the audit log.

Last step: point the desktop app at your server. Then we'll see how the audit log records who did what.

  1. Add a server profile

    Welcome screen or Settings › Profiles › Add profile › Dispatch server.

    In the app, add a new profile, and choose Dispatch server.

  2. Sign in

    Enter the server URL, your email and password, then press ↵. Or paste a token from your admin.

    Enter the server address, and the email and password your admin gave you, then press Enter. Or paste a token instead.

  3. Work as usual

    Your granted workspaces appear. Everything syncs through the server.

    And you're in. Your granted workspaces appear, and everything works just like before, only now it goes through your team's server.

  4. Read the audit log

    Admin panel › Audit log: who did what, and when. Search and filter it.

    Back in the admin panel, the Audit log shows every call and every account change: who did it, what it was, and when. Search it, or filter by status.

That's the whole team set up: one server, the right access for everyone, and a record of every change.
