Work faster: palette, history and cookies

Jump anywhere with the command palette, reopen past sends, and manage cookies.

Let's speed things up. A few everyday tools will save you a lot of clicks: the command palette, history, and the cookie manager.

  1. Search everything

    Press ⌘+K. Find any request, folder, environment or tab by name or URL.

    Press Command K, or Control K, to open the command palette. Type part of a name or a URL, and jump straight to it.

  2. Run actions

    Start with > to see actions only: new request, import, cookies, switch workspace.

    Start with a greater than sign, to see actions only. New request, import, cookies, switching workspace, all from the keyboard.

  3. Reopen past sends

    History keeps your last 300 sends, grouped by day.

    The History tab keeps your last 300 sends, grouped by day. Click one to reopen it, exactly as it was sent.

  4. Manage cookies

    Cookies are kept and sent back for you. Open the manager from Settings, the response menu, or ⌘+K.

    Cookies from responses are kept, and sent back automatically. Open the cookie manager to see them by domain, and add, edit or delete them.

  5. Shortcuts worth knowing

    ⌘+↵ send · ⌘+S save · ⌘+T new tab · ⌘+W close · ⌘+L URL bar

    And a few shortcuts worth learning: ⌘ Enter to send, ⌘ S to save, ⌘ T for a new tab, and ⌘ L to jump to the URL bar.

Those small tools add up to a lot of saved time. Next, we'll sync with Postman.
