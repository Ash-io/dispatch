Two-way sync with Postman

Work in the same workspaces as teammates who use Postman, in both directions.

Your team uses Postman? No problem. Dispatch syncs both ways with your Postman workspaces, so you can use Dispatch while they keep using Postman.

  1. Create a Postman API key

    In Postman: avatar › Settings › API keys › Generate API key. It starts with PMAK-.

    In Postman, open your settings, then API keys, and generate a new key. Copy it. It starts with PMAK.

  2. Connect it

    Paste the key and click Connect. EU team? Tick the EU box first.

    Back in Dispatch, paste the key and click Connect. If your team is on Postman's EU region, tick that box first.

  3. Pick a workspace

    Switch workspaces from the top of the sidebar. Collections and environments load in.

    Choose a workspace from the top of the sidebar. Its collections and environments load in, just as they are in Postman.

  4. Save pushes to Postman

    Edit and press ⌘+S. The change goes straight to Postman.

    Now edit a request, and save it. The change goes straight back to Postman, where your teammates see it.

  5. Pull their changes

    Click ↻, or just come back to the window. Teammates' edits are pulled in.

    To get your teammates' changes, click the sync button. Dispatch also syncs on its own, whenever the window comes back into focus.

  6. Conflicts are caught

    If it changed in Postman since you synced, choose: overwrite, or load theirs.

    And if someone changed the same collection since you last synced, Dispatch asks first. Keep your version, or load theirs. Your edits stay in your open tabs, so nothing is lost.

You and your Postman teammates now share the same work, both ways. Next, we'll set up a shared Dispatch server for your team.
